# Dirigo RC Brand Guidelines

Working notes for website updates, flyers, race graphics, sponsor materials, and social posts.

Dirigo should feel like a Maine racing club with history: fast, grounded, direct, communal, and proudly blue. The look is athletic, but not corporate. It should feel made by runners for runners.

## Brand Idea

Dirigo brings Maine runners together to train hard, race as a team, and represent the state well.

Useful language:

- Race hard. Train together.
- Maine running club. Competitive since 2002.
- For Maine runners who want more from racing.
- Race, train, and carry the club.
- Wear the blue.
- Built for Maine racing.

Avoid:

- Corporate wellness language.
- Over-explaining operational details.
- Generic running-stock-photo energy.
- Copy that makes the club feel closed, elite-only, or overly bureaucratic.

## Logo System

Primary marks:

- Wordmark: `assets/images/dirigo-wordmark-min.png`
- Full state/tree mark, white: `assets/images/dirigo-logo-white-min.png`
- Full state/tree mark, blue: `assets/images/dirigo-logo.png`
- Extended underline tile: `assets/images/dirigo-wordmark-underline.png`

Use the wordmark when the piece needs to feel classic, official, or header-like. Use the state/tree mark when the piece needs a badge, stamp, or race-singlet signal.

Logo guidance:

- Give the wordmark room. It looks best large, centered, or left-aligned with confidence.
- The extended underline can run off the left edge for a fun, site-native detail.
- Use the white logo on Dirigo blue, black, or dark photography.
- Use the blue logo on white or very light backgrounds.
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows, recolor into non-brand colors, or place over busy photo areas without a dark overlay.

## Color

Core palette:

| Role | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dirigo Blue | `#0057b8` | Primary brand color, buttons, header, date blocks, rules |
| Dark Blue | `#002f6c` | Photo overlays, deeper blue fields |
| Ink | `#05070a` | Black backgrounds, headlines, footer |
| White | `#ffffff` | Main background, reversed text |
| Off White | `#f3f7fb` | Soft section backgrounds |
| Ice | `#e9f2ff` | Pale blue accent fields |
| Muted Gray | `#5d6573` | Supporting copy |
| Line Gray | `#d8dee8` | Borders and dividers |

Recommended color ratios:

- Most pieces should be blue, white, and black first.
- Use pale blue/off-white as breathing room, not as the main identity.
- Let photography add natural secondary colors.
- Avoid one-note blue gradients, beige/tan palettes, and decorative color blobs.

## Typography

Display type:

- Font: `Mandrel Ext`
- Style: black italic, all caps
- Use for big headlines, section titles, short calls to action, and race-poster moments.

Body type:

- Font: Inter or system sans-serif
- Use for all readable copy, event details, captions, results, names, and small UI.

Typography rules:

- Big display headlines should be short and forceful.
- Keep letter spacing at `0`.
- Use uppercase for display headlines and small labels.
- Avoid using the display font for long sentences or dense informational text.
- When in doubt, make the headline more compact and let the body copy carry nuance.

Good headline shapes:

- RACE HARD. TRAIN TOGETHER.
- FOR MAINE RUNNERS WHO WANT MORE FROM RACING.
- RECENT FROM THE BLUE CREW.
- ON THE CALENDAR.
- NO TIME STANDARD. JUST A TEAM WORTH SHOWING UP FOR.

## Photography

Photography is essential. The brand should show real Dirigo runners, real races, real Maine roads/trails/tracks, and the blue singlet.

Use:

- Race photos with visible effort, teamwork, starts, finishes, packs, or podiums.
- Team photos in singlets.
- Maine-specific settings: roads, coast, Back Cove, trails, tracks, Beach to Beacon, local race scenes.
- Historical photos when talking about the club’s past.

Avoid:

- Generic stock running photos.
- Overly polished corporate fitness imagery.
- Crops that cut off faces, bibs, or the Dirigo singlet when those are the point.
- Blurry/dark images unless the mood is intentional and the text remains legible.

Photo treatment:

- Use full-bleed or large photos when possible.
- Put white text over photos only with a strong blue/black overlay.
- Grayscale historical photos can work well with black, white, and blue.
- Always credit photographers where known.

## Layout

The current site uses a few repeatable composition moves that should also work for flyers.

### Hero Layout

Best for event posters, social graphics, and major announcements.

- Large photo background.
- Dark blue/black overlay.
- White Dirigo mark.
- Short all-caps Mandrel headline.
- Small blue or white eyebrow line.
- One clear action or detail block.

Example:

```text
[photo with blue/black overlay]

Dirigo mark
MAINE RUNNING CLUB | COMPETITIVE SINCE 2002

RACE HARD.
TRAIN TOGETHER.

Saturday long run. 7:00 AM. Payson Park.
```

### Split Layout

Best for flyers with a little more information.

- Left: huge Mandrel headline.
- Right: concise sans-serif details.
- Add one strong blue rule or left border.

Example:

```text
RUN,
SHOP,
AND SHOW UP
FOR THE TEAM.

Saturday, August 8
8:00-10:00 AM
Coastal Run Maine
Coffee, donuts, raffle, and Dirigo member discount.
```

### Card Layout

Use cards sparingly, mainly for repeated information.

- 8px radius maximum.
- Subtle border.
- Blue date block, stat, or label.
- Not every section needs to be a card.

Good card uses:

- Calendar event.
- Race result recap.
- Three-step membership/info block.

Avoid:

- Cards inside cards.
- Decorative cards that just make the layout busier.

## Graphic Details

Signature elements:

- Thick Dirigo blue rules.
- Black feature bands.
- White or pale-blue small uppercase labels.
- Blue date blocks.
- Underlined text links.
- The wordmark underline extending off the left edge.

Borders and radius:

- Radius: `8px`
- Strong left borders: 5-10px Dirigo blue.
- Dividers: black for major section breaks, line gray for content rows.

Buttons and calls to action:

- Blue filled button for the primary action.
- White outlined button on dark/photo backgrounds.
- Uppercase, bold sans-serif.
- Keep labels short: `Join Dirigo`, `Register`, `See Results`, `Full Calendar`.

## Voice

Dirigo copy should be plainspoken, active, and a little proud.

Use:

- “Dirigo runners…”
- “The blue singlet…”
- “The team…”
- “Maine runners…”
- “Show up,” “line up,” “race,” “train,” “carry the club.”

Avoid:

- “Experience a world-class community…”
- “Unlock your potential…”
- “Athletes of all levels embarking on a journey…”
- Long caveats in public-facing copy.

Good tone:

> Dirigo runners have made Back Cove a steady weekly check-in for racing, fitness, and teammates.

Less good:

> Dirigo has had strong early-summer turnout at Back Cove, making the series a steady weekly check-in for racing, fitness, and teammates.

Shorter usually wins.

## Flyer Recipes

### Race Result Graphic

Use when celebrating a result.

- Photo first.
- Small label: `RESULTS`
- Stat line: `17:13 5K | 1ST WOMAN`
- Headline: runner/result in one sentence.
- Body: one or two lines max.
- Link or footer: race name, photo credit, Dirigo logo.

### Calendar Event Flyer

Use when promoting an upcoming race or team event.

- Big date block in Dirigo blue.
- Event title in bold sans-serif or Mandrel, depending on space.
- Time, location, registration link.
- Team note in plain language.
- Dirigo wordmark or state/tree mark.

### Team/Sponsor Flyer

Use for Coastal Run Maine or partner items.

- Dirigo blue or black frame.
- Partner logo on a clean white/off-white panel.
- Short benefit copy.
- Avoid making the sponsor block look more important than Dirigo unless the piece is explicitly sponsor-led.

## Social Guidelines

Instagram post:

- Square or 4:5 vertical is safest.
- Big photo with short headline.
- Keep small text minimal.
- Use carousel posts when results are dense.

Instagram story:

- Strong photo crop.
- Dirigo logo top or bottom.
- Big result/stat.
- Keep copy large enough to read on a phone.

Website update image:

- Prefer real race photo.
- Avoid over-cropping; let mixed aspect ratios breathe.
- Credit photographers in the update links or caption.

## Accessibility And Practical Notes

- Keep contrast high, especially white text on photo overlays.
- Do not put important text over faces or bibs.
- Make dates, times, and locations easy to find.
- Use full member names in public copy.
- Include photo credits when known.
- For event flyers, include registration/status details only when confirmed.

## Quick Checklist

Before publishing a flyer or graphic:

- Does it use Dirigo blue, white, and black clearly?
- Is the Dirigo mark visible and not distorted?
- Is the headline short and strong?
- Is the photo real and relevant?
- Are date, time, location, and action obvious?
- Are names complete and spelled correctly?
- Are photo credits included where needed?
- Does it feel like a Maine racing club, not a generic fitness brand?
