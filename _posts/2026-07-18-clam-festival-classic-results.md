Dirigo had a strong morning in Yarmouth at the Pat's Pizza Clam Festival Classic 5 Miler.

Alexis Wilbert led the way, winning the women's race in 27:29 and placing 10th overall. Eric Sofen was eighth overall in 26:58 and won M40-49, while Ryan Morrison was 11th in 27:45.

Robert Ashby kept rolling with another age-group win, taking M50-59 in 28:19. Nedicus Swain ran 29:14, Kevin Murray ran 30:42, and Paul Greene was fifth in M50-59 in 33:50.
