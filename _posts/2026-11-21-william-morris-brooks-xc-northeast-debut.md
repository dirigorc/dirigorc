In his Dirigo debut, high school standout William Morris finished 12th in the Brooks Northeast Regional in New Jersey, covering the 5k course in 16:05 and only two spots off qualifying for Nationals. Joining him and making the trip, James Vailancourt (NYA) and Stetson Thurston (Dirigo) also made their Dirigo debuts, with James running a 17:08, and Stetson finishing 13th in the Jr/Sr boys race in 18:43. What a debut day for these boys!
