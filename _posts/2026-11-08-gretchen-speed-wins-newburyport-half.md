A group of Dirigo women went down to Newburyport on November 8th, and Gretchen Speed WON the women’s half marathon in a time of 1:23:57. A long-time Dirigo veteran, Gretchen is now 45 and still posting performances like these. Mary Iaculli was the 3rd woman overall in 1:26:28, and Kalie Dunn was 3rd in the women’s 40-44 age group and 49th overall in 1:33:32.
