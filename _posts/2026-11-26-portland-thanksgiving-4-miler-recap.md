The team showed up and showed out in Portland on Thanksgiving! Bethanie Brown was on a tear, taking the women’s win in 21:24 and Dirigo women took the next 3 places, with Veronica Graziano in 22:06, Taylor Bagley (a 40-second PR for her) in 22:43, and Michelle Lilienthal (also the Masters winner at age 44) in 23:05. Grady Satterfield was 2nd overall in 18:50, and Justin Tomison also up front in 5th overall at 20:30. Seth Crockett was 18th in 22:27 and 2nd in his age group. Georgia Moon (F15-19), Ed Demetriou (M50-54), Mary Pardi (F55-59), Gretchen Speed (F45-49), Pete Bottomley (M65-69) and Charles Kaufmann (M70-74) all won their age groups. Kelly Brown (F60-64) and Marah Borgman (F40-44) were 2nd in theirs, and Paul Greene (M50-54), Jorma Kurry (M55-59), and Scott Brown (M65-69) 3rd in theirs. It was a big day all-around. This year’s race again had over 1,000 runners, and was the first year without longtime race director and founder George Towle.
