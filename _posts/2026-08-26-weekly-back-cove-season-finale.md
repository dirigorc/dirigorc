The Weekly Back Cove Series ended on Wednesday with the final race of the summer. Cliodhna O'Malley took the Overall Women's Series Win and came out and ran 17:33 for the final race, of course winning yet again. Marah Borgman won the female 40-49 again this summer - going away.

Nick Denari again took the overall men's win for another season, with a low-16 average. He racked up numerous wins.

And Dirigo Youth Enzo Giampaolo, soon to be bound for Dartmouth to continue his running, took the win in the summer's final race, winning comfortably in low 16’s.

Dave Ouellette, Deven Abrams, Nevin Duffey, Crow Norlander, Adane Bewset, Ryan Tuttle, and Seth Crockett all scored in the season standings.
