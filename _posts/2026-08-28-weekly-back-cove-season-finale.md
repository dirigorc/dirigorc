The Weekly Back Cove Series closed out the summer on Wednesday, Aug. 26. Clíodhna O'Malley — who had already wrapped the overall women's series title — still came out and won the final race in 17:33, while Nick Denari locked up another strong season to take the men's overall championship.

Marah Borgman repeated as the female 40-49 series winner, and Dirigo youth Enzo Giampaolo won the final race (Enzo wore a Coastal singlet and turned in another fast outing after posting mid-16s at earlier nights this summer). Club members filled the standings across divisions: season scorers included Dave Ouellette, Deven Abrams, Nevin Duffey, Crow Norlander, Adane Bewset, and Ryan Tuttle. Dan and Levi Bannon, Katie Mauro, Chris Harmon, Grace Alexander, Grace Beeler, and Allie Schmitt also made regular appearances through the series. On the women's side Bethanie Brown, Aly Ursiny, and Veronica Graziano all raced multiple nights.

Organizers published a full set of results and the season standings online; the standings page shows the complete list of qualifiers (the series reported 255 qualifying finishers this year). Thanks to everyone who made Wednesday nights a reliable local highlight — lots of blue singlets and lots of consistent effort.

(Separate note: Heather Gallant's Triple Crown win at the L-A Bridge Run was covered in a standalone post; see the site archives for her course-record run on Aug. 23.)
