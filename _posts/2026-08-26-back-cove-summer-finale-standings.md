Wednesday’s Weekly Back Cove Series wrapped up on Aug. 26 with familiar blue at the front and strong season finishes across age groups.

Clíodhna O'Malley—who had already clinched the overall women's series—ran the final race in 17:33 and scored another win to close the summer. Nick Denari repeated as the overall men's series champion after another consistent season of front-of-pack finishes.

Marah Borgman again owned the female 40–49 standings, taking the age-group season title by a wide margin. Youth runner Enzo Giampaolo won the final race of the series; he’s heading to Dartmouth this fall and ran in the mid-16s during the summer races.

Season scorers from Dirigo included Dave Ouellette, Deven Abrams, Nevin Duffey, Crow Norlander, Adane Bewset, and Ryan Tuttle—each ran enough races to qualify in the standings. Daniel and Levi Bannon made multiple appearances through the series, and Katie Mauro, Chris Harmon, Grace Alexander, Grace Beeler, and Allie Schmitt all showed up through the summer. On the women's side, Bethanie Brown, Aly Ursiny, and Veronica Graziano also contributed strong performances across the season; Bethanie picked up at least one weekly win.

The series qualified 255 runners in the final standings; by the organizer’s count many Dirigo members placed inside the top quarter of qualifiers. Full race results and season standings are available from the Back Cove results and standings pages linked above.
