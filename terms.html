Dirigo RC

Terms

How to use this site, and what annual membership covers.

This website is the public home of Dirigo, a Maine running club founded in 2002. Race reports, photos, and calendar notes are published by the club for members and other Maine runners.

Membership

Adult membership is $40 per year. Registration and payment happen on RunSignup, not on this site. After you register, the club can follow up with team communications, singlet size, and setup details.

Membership is for runners with a Maine connection who will train, race, and treat teammates and other athletes with good sportsmanship. There is no time standard. A specific race or scoring opportunity can still set its own entry rules.

Other sites

Links to RunSignup, Coastal Run Maine, Instagram, Facebook, and Strava leave this site. Those organizations set their own terms. Coastal Run Maine discount details are described on the join page and can change.

Photos and results

Race photos stay credited to the photographer named on the page. Results are club reports of public race outcomes. If a result or photo credit is wrong, message Dirigo on Instagram at @dirigorc and the club will correct it.
