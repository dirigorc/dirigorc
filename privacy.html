Dirigo RC

Privacy

What this website records, and what it leaves to the services that actually handle membership.

Dirigo is a Maine running club. This site publishes club news, the calendar, and membership information. It does not take payment, and it does not store a membership account.

Analytics

The site loads three analytics tools: Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, and Cloudflare Web Analytics. They record pages visited, general device and browser details, and approximate location derived from the network address. Clarity can also record anonymized interaction patterns such as clicks and scrolls.

Those tools set their own cookies or similar storage. You can block them in the browser. The club uses the reports to see which pages are useful, not to build a profile of individual runners.

Membership

Join and renew links go to RunSignup. RunSignup collects the registration details and the $40 annual dues. Dirigo receives what RunSignup shares with the club so a representative can follow up about singlet size and team communications.

Contact

Questions about this page can go to Dirigo on Instagram at @dirigorc.
