## Design and checks

Three small compositions were rendered: illustration, typography, and authentic group photography. References inspected: the existing Merry Miles flyer, the August Coastal Run shop graphic, and the club history graphic. Observed devices include oversized italic headlines, blue/white contrast, large team photography and clean logo fields. The illustrated concept was chosen for its holiday-specific focal point and athletic blue silhouette. `concepts.jpg` records initial exploration, not additional final exports.

Final refinement separated the shoe/hat silhouette from the title, increased practical text sizes, and shortened the supporting subtitle. Final PNG opened at full resolution and at 390px phone width; checked factual copy, date, logos, margins, drawing construction, and clipping. No decorative emphasis rays or shine marks. White on Dirigo Blue and blue on Ice exceed 4.5:1 contrast. Screen social export only; no print PDF requested. Not published or sent.
